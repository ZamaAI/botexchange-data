What it is

Pump.fun is a Solana launchpad. Anyone can mint a memecoin for free; it trades on a bonding curve until the curve fills, then liquidity moves to the PumpSwap AMM. Creators and short-horizon traders pay trading fees. PUMP is not needed to use the product and gives no claim on revenue; the project says half of platform revenue buys and burns it.
