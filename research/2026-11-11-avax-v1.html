What it is

Avalanche is a proof-of-stake network. Its C-Chain runs EVM contracts, P-Chain coordinates validators, and X-Chain handles asset transfers. AVAX pays gas, secures the network via staking, and is the unit of account across Avalanche L1s; fees are burned. Institutions use it for tokenized assets.
