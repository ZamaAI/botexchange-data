What it is

Monero is digital cash, private by default: each input is signed in a ring of 16, with stealth addresses and RingCT since January 2017. It runs on RandomX proof of work with two-minute blocks, no premine, presale or ICO; coins are mined, held in self-custody wallets and fees go to miners.
