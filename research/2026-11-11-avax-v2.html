What it is

Avalanche is a layer-1 blockchain. AVAX pays fees, secures the network through staking and is the unit of account across Avalanche L1s; the C-Chain runs Ethereum-compatible contracts and the P-Chain coordinates validators and custom L1s. Institutions use it for tokenised assets, settlement and payments; Schwab made AVAX available through Schwab Crypto on 9 Nov 2026.
