What it is

NEAR is a layer-1 blockchain whose main product is NEAR Intents, a solver-based cross-chain swap service that settles on NEAR: a user states a goal, professional solvers bid within about a second, and settlement happens on NEAR. Chain Signatures let a NEAR account sign on Bitcoin, Ethereum or Solana without a bridge.
