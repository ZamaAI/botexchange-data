What it is

NVDA is Robinhood's tokenised NVIDIA stock on CoinGecko id nvidia-robinhood-tokenized-stock. It is not equity. Issuer documents describe a debt note that pays the NVDA price, so holders get 24/7 price exposure and dividend reinvestment through a multiplier, without shareholder rights, and redeem only for cash through an approved dealer.
