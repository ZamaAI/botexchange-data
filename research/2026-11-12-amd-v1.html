Method

Every number is read by the bot from the named source at the time shown; the AI (deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash, with web search) writes the words and may only use those numbers — a gate checks every figure in the text before publication. The verdict starts from the research's stated lean. 9 independent reads of the same research then answer, item by item, whether valuation, results and estimate revisions each make a lower or a higher price more likely eight weeks out; the price trend is asked too but is not counted. The direction is kept only when at least two of those three items support it — 7 of the 9 reads agreeing on each — and none points the other way; a dated event that could go against it lowers that by one step. Anything else is published as NEUTRAL, with the tilt and the count stated. The range is the share's 30-session realised volatility projected 8 weeks. Trade levels follow fixed price rules: the entry zone sits at the edge of the recent range (no chasing), the stop one weekly 1σ beyond it, Target 1 at twice the risk; no trade is opened in the 7 calendar days (five trading sessions) before an earnings report.
