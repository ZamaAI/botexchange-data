What it is

Ethereum is a shared computer: thousands of independent nodes run the same smart-contract code, so programs can hold money and enforce rules without a company. ETH pays gas, is posted as a validator deposit under proof of stake, and is used as money and collateral, serving stablecoin rails, DeFi lenders, tokenised-asset issuers and rollups that settle to mainnet.
