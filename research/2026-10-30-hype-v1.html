What it is

Hyperliquid is a Layer 1 blockchain whose main application is a fully on-chain order-book exchange for perpetual futures and spot. Users trade perps, stake HYPE to secure the network for fee discounts and pay gas on HyperEVM. The core users are professional market makers, prop traders and whales needing low latency and no gas on HyperCore.
