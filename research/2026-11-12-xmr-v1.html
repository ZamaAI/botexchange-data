What it is

Monero is a proof-of-work payments chain where every transaction hides sender, amount and receiver by default, using ring signatures, RingCT and stealth addresses. It mines with RandomX on ordinary CPUs, one block about every two minutes. Its users are people who need money to move without a public trail: remittance corridors, merchants settling through swap services, and privacy-first savers.

Supply: No unlocks; miners get new coins

Nothing is scheduled to unlock over the next 90 days: all supply already circulates and new coins go only to miners as block rewards. Tail emission has been fixed at 0.6 XMR per two-minute block since June 2022, and daily issuance ran about 293–446 XMR over 4–11 Nov 2026.

Valuation: Fees are the only multiple

Fees are the right yardstick for a Layer 1 whose holders have no claim on protocol income; revenue is zero, so a revenue multiple cannot be drawn at all. Across the fee peer set the range runs from 41.0× (Polygon) to 1016.5× (Ethereum), and XMR sits above the top of it.
