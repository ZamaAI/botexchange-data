What it is

ETHFI is the governance token of ether.fi, a crypto app offering ETH staking through eETH/weETH, automated yield vaults, a Visa card called Cash and Markets trading. Users stake ETH, spend via the card and borrow against crypto; ETHFI is used for votes and app membership perks.
